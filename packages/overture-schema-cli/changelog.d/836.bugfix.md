Fixed `overture-schema validate` error reports for segments, divisions and other features
with a `subtype`. A road segment's field errors keep their messages instead of being shown
as a missing field (`road <missing>`), and no feature's field errors are hidden behind, or
reported as ambiguous with, an `Input tag ... does not match` error from a union of types
that does not apply to it. A tag error from inside a feature, such as a road segment's
`vehicle` selector with an unknown `dimension`, is reported like any other error. A base
`water` feature is reported as `Water` and a water segment as `WaterSegment`, whichever
types are selected.
